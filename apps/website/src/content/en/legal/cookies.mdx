title: Cookies
description: This website sets no cookies. It stores one optional setting in your browser, your colour theme, and nothing else.
updated: 2026-11-24
---

This website sets **no cookies**, so there is no cookie banner.

It stores one setting in your browser's local storage: your colour theme (Auto, Light or Dark), if you choose one in the footer. It never leaves your browser.

The spam check on our forms (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs only when you start filling in a form.
