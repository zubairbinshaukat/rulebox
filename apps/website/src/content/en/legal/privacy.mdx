This notice covers this website only. The Rulebox product and the hosted dashboard will have their own notice.

## What this website collects

- **When you read pages:** nothing that identifies you. We set no cookies and use no advertising or tracking pixels. If we turn on Cloudflare Web Analytics, it counts page views without cookies or local storage.
- **When you send a form:** the fields you fill in (for example your name, work email and organisation on the pilot form), sent by email to the Rulebox team so we can reply.
- **When you ask to be notified:** your email address, kept on a mailing list until you unsubscribe.

## Who processes it

- **Cloudflare** hosts the site, runs the spam check (Turnstile) and handles the form request.
- **Resend** sends the emails.

Form submissions are not stored in a database by this website.

## Your choices

Ask us to see or delete what you sent: use the [contact page](/contact).
