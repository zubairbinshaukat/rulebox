Rulebox is an open-source kiosk OS for Windows. It turns an ordinary PC into a locked, branded workstation. Staff sign in and can use only the apps, websites and files you approve. You manage everything from a web dashboard.

## The three parts

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **The agent** | A small Windows service that enforces your rules, installs apps and keeps the desktop alive. |
| **The staff desktop** | What people see: sign-in, taskbar, Start menu, approved apps and their own files. |
| **The dashboard** | Where you manage PCs, people, apps and rules from any browser. |

## Does it replace Windows?

No. Rulebox runs on top of Windows. It replaces the Windows desktop for one special "Staff" account and locks that account down. The administrator account stays normal Windows.

## Desired state, not commands

You never send a PC a list of commands. You change the rules in the dashboard, and each PC moves to the latest signed version of its rules. A PC that was offline does not replay a backlog; it goes straight to the current state when it reconnects.

## What it is not for

Unattended single-app screens such as digital signage, ticket machines and self-checkout. Windows' built-in single-app kiosk mode is made for that. Rulebox is for people who sign in and work.
