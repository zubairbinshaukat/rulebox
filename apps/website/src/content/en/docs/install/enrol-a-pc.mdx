Enrolment ties a PC to your organisation. After it, the PC pulls its apps and rules by itself.

## Steps

1. **Install.** Run the installer on the PC. It checks the PC first and shows the protection level it can reach.
2. **Enrol.** Type the enrolment code from your dashboard.
3. **Manage.** The PC restarts as a Rulebox workstation: your name, your logo, your apps. Add people and apps in the dashboard; the PC follows.

## About enrolment codes

Enrolment codes are short-lived, and you can revoke one from the dashboard.

## A new branch or a new PC

Install, type the code, done. The PC pulls its apps and rules by itself, and reports back to the dashboard.
